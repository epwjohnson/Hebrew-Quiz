The High Holy Days

יָמִים נוֹרָאִים ya-MIM no-ra-IM
the Days of Awe

Ten days in autumn, from Rosh Hashanah to the close of Yom Kippur, set aside for looking back, making amends and beginning again. These guides follow our own machzorim page by page, so you can find your way through the services, read the key prayers in Hebrew and understand them.

1 Tishrei

Rosh Hashanah, the Day of Memorial

יוֹם הַזִּכָּרוֹן yom ha-zi-ka-RON

The new year: the sound of the shofar, G_d as Ruler of the world, and the Binding of Isaac.

The journey through the season

  1. Elul the month before A month of preparation. Psalm 27, "The Lord is my light", is said every day.
  2. Rosh Hashanah 1 Tishrei The shofar calls us to remember.
  3. Ten Days of Return 1–10 Tishrei Time to put things right.
  4. Yom Kippur 10 Tishrei Beginning with Kol Nidrei.
  5. Ne'ilah the close of the day The gates close; one long shofar call.

The Ten Days of Return עֲשֶׂרֶת יְמֵי תְשׁוּבָה a-SE-ret ye-MEI te-shu-VA

The days between are called the Ten Days of Return. Some prayers change for the whole period, and many texts from Rosh Hashanah come back on Yom Kippur.
